    How it works

    Our own model. Opponent-adjusted team ratings for every league, refit on each run from completed games, blended with the sportsbook line. That gives a fair chance for every winner, spread and total.

    Who we are trying to beat. The sportsbooks. Every game shows the Vegas line, the Kil Cap line and the gap between them, and every bet shows our chance next to the Vegas chance with the vig removed. The test of the model is whether it is right more often than that line.

    Where the bet goes. Kalshi. It is the place and the price, not the opponent. A side only becomes a bet when Kalshi's price, after its taker fee (7% × price × (1 − price)), is below our fair price. Gaps over 15% are held out, because they are almost always bad data rather than value.

    Ranking and stakes. Overall rank is expected profit. Stakes are quarter-Kelly on your bankroll, capped by your max bet and your budget, one bet per game. Win-majority (55%+) is on by default.

    Every game gets a call. If a game has no bet, the board says why: Kalshi's price is too high, the value is too small, or our number is too far from Vegas to trust.

    Learning. Each night, after 4 AM Central, a challenger model is tested on games it never saw. It replaces the champion only if it predicts better. Results are graded against the price we would have paid.
